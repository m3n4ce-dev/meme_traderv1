# T14-TP10: a 25% trim at +10% net on graduation plays (PROPOSED, draft 1)

**Status: proposed, not registered.** It's the ninth review's protocol (answer 5). The thresholds marked *owner* need the owner's decision before any registration.

- **Until then,** the TP10 variants run only as exploratory exit-lab shadows (`hypotheses.csv` T14), and **no live exit changes.**

## The one primary candidate

**For the `late` (graduation-play) entry family only:**
- trim **25%** when a fresh executable full-position liquidation quote gives **≥ +10% total net P&L**;
- the rest stays on the frozen baseline exits.

**Why the trim:** it may keep the rare winners that the full +10% exit gives away. The reviewer's generous fixed-entry check had the full exit turning `late`'s +0.33 SOL into −7.07 SOL.

**Diagnostics only, never promoted after the fact:**
- "all out at +10% net";
- +10% on the price;
- the KOL-sell exits;
- the late-landing variants.

## Prerequisites (before the window starts)

1. **The exit lab's single cash ledger.** Done: round 9, accounting version 2.
2. **Realistic exits.** Done in the lab: delayed exits that keep their fraction, failed attempts with retries, every cost counted, and the price followed after an early exit.
3. **The executable quote.** The PumpSwap quote module exists (`pumpswap.py`, matching the SDK exactly); the live observer that feeds it does not, yet.
4. **Two policy accounts.** The baseline and the candidate are constrained separately, with re-entries and all rejected opportunities, and a shared opportunity stream.
5. **Calibrated null and power** under clustering, heavy tails, missing exits and execution costs, all **before** freezing the window.

## Protocol

- **The window:** **60** complete future UTC days, after qualification and the version freeze.
- **Sample floor:**
  - ≥ 200 eligible executed entry comparisons, over ≥ 100 distinct pool × 2-hour episodes and ≥ 30 active days;
  - these aren't assumed independent: inference is by day block.
- **Coverage:** both policies must price ≥ 95% of exits directly (proposed). The rest are conservatively impaired in the primary P&L, and failures, fees and coverage are reported for each arm.
- **A missed floor or coverage gate** means INCONCLUSIVE or INVALID. It's never extended until it passes.
- **Primary endpoint:**
  - incremental end-of-window net cash P&L over the frozen baseline;
  - same 9 SOL bank, sizes and limits;
  - deposits and resets excluded;
  - unresolved inventory valued conservatively.
- **The hurdle H:** *proposed* **0.5 SOL over 60 days** (*owner*). It's an acceptance threshold, not a forecast.
- **Promotion requires all three:**
  - the one-sided 95% lower bound of incremental mean daily economic P&L > 0 (3-day block resampling, paired days, zero-trade days kept; 7-day blocks as a sensitivity);
  - the observed incremental total ≥ H;
  - the candidate's absolute net P&L > 0. The baseline losing more isn't enough.
- **Downside:** the marked, conservatively impaired account drawdown is no worse than the baseline's by more than *proposed* 0.5 SOL (*owner*).
- **Reported:**
  - the largest-episode and leave-one-episode-out sensitivities;
  - rejection differences;
  - the right-tail upside forfeited;
  - turnover and fees.

  The biggest winner is never deleted from the primary endpoint.
