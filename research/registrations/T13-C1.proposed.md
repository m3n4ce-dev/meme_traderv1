# T13-C1: does a frozen wallet's buy add value to momentum? (PROPOSED, draft 1)

**Status: proposed, not registered.** Drafted from an eighth review's answer 4 (2026-10-07). Nothing is built for it, and it needs the owner's approval. Once approved and built, this file is frozen (hash-locked like `research/policies/*`); any material change is a new version with its own untouched window.

**It depends on three things that don't exist yet:**
1. **A frozen wallets-v1 list.** wallets-v1's period A began 2026-10-04 and can't freeze before 2026-10-18. There's no frozen list yet.
2. **The replayed wallet ledger** (`copytrade.LeaderBook`, fixed in the eighth review), plus as-of opening balances for the frozen wallets.
3. **A qualified quote observer** (the same one T9-E1 needs).

## The question

> Does verified buying by previously selected wallets improve the net economic outcome over momentum alone, on matched pools and cash, at our real size, information delay and execution delay?

- **C (primary):** one verified buy by a frozen wallets-v1 wallet, followed within a predeclared lookback by the frozen T9 momentum condition (`t9_signal.py`, `t9-signal-2`).
- **A (baseline):** the same momentum condition alone.
- **B (diagnostic):** the wallet's buy alone.

**The primary test is C minus A.** Neither A nor B can earn a confirmatory PASS, and the winner among the three is not picked after the fact.

## Selection and provenance

- **Wallets:** wallets-v1's frozen list, exactly as frozen.
  - Saved with this registration: the wallet IDs, the selection window's bounds, the rules' hash (`904875fa93aa`) and the freeze time.
  - Never pruned or rotated on period-B or observer results. "Winners of period B" are never copied into C.
- **No new discovery period** is needed to reuse a genuinely frozen list. If C ever needs other discovery rules or filters, that's a new policy with its own selection window. wallets-v1 isn't edited.
- **The window:** C's confirmatory window starts after wallets-v1's period B ends (about 2026-11-01). Implementation and a shadow run can happen before it, and shadow results never count.

## The rule (constants marked *TBD* get fixed before the freeze, from operations or a separate exploratory period, never from the outcomes used for the verdict)

- **Wallet buy:**
  - The buy must be verified: a decoded on-chain event whose fork evidence is final.
  - Minimum size: *TBD*, relative to the pool's executable depth at our stake.
- **Then momentum:** the T9 condition on the same pool within a lookback of *TBD* minutes after the buy.
- **Universe and gates:** the same as T9-E1:
  - established PumpSwap pools;
  - no Mayhem coins (an unknown status is ineligible);
  - the feed-lag skip;
  - the quote validity contract.
- **Execution:** the same as T9-E1: decision at read time, then the delay, a fresh quote, the impact gate, the stake, and the one-hour exit with its retries.
- **Account:** a separate 9 SOL paper account. It runs the same `t9_portfolio` contract: economic primary P&L, impairments, quarantine and the risk budget.
- **Cooldown:** *TBD*.
- **"Independent wallets":** when funding relationships are unknown, the feature carries that uncertainty. It's never assumed independent.

## Information availability

- **Feature versions:** every feature uses only versions with `available_at <= decision time`. Chain time isn't availability time.
- **Inventory:**
  - A wallet starts from an as-of token-account balance snapshot. Then buys, sells, transfers and corrections are processed.
  - A token balance isn't a cost basis. An unknown opening cost stays unknown.
  - Observed trade flow is kept apart from full inventory and from the wallet's profit.
- **The wallet's own profit isn't ours.** A public wallet's return isn't the copier's attainable return. The observer observes and ranks; it doesn't trade.

## Comparator and verdict

- **Comparison:** C and A run on separate identical accounts, on the same clock and pool universe, with the same costs, missing-state rules and risk rules.
- **Cash** is the second baseline.
- **Controls:** each C signal gets a matched pool, following the review's answer 3:
  - one primary control;
  - momentum aligned between the arms, since the wallet is the feature under test;
  - pre-decision baselines;
  - seeded, predeclared calipers;
  - an unmatched signal is recorded as such.
- **Primary statistic:** the chronological difference in economic daily P&L, C minus A. It's bootstrapped by day, with a multi-day block sensitivity.
- **PASS / FAIL / INCONCLUSIVE / INVALID:** structured as in T9-E1, with its own H and window. Both are chosen from corrected power after the quote observer qualifies.
- **Reported alongside:**
  - B and A results;
  - winner concentration;
  - missed upside;
  - coverage by reason;
  - wallet-feature unknown rates.
