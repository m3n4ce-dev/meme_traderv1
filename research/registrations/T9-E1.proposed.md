# T9-E1: second-life momentum, one executable configuration (PROPOSED, draft 5)

**Status: proposed, not registered.** It needs the owner's approval, and nothing is built for it yet. Once approved and built, this file is frozen (hash-locked like `research/policies/*`), and any material change starts T9-E2 with its own untouched period. The existing forward test (`revival.py`, v2, 18 variants) stays **exploratory**: its numbers are never quoted as confirmation of this one.

**Draft 5 (2026-10-07)** applies a ninth review:
- **Exits fill when a quote exists.** An exit filled by a later retry is booked at the fill's time, day and return. The position holds its cash and is a failed exit, with risk reserved, from the due time until then. Power v3 had credited such exits at the intended time, inside the outage that delayed them.
- **Both arms must reach the coverage gate,** signal and control.
- **The quote module exists:** `meme_trader/sniper/pumpswap.py`, which matches the official SDK 1.20.0 exactly on 94 cases. Real mainnet Pool accounts are 287–301 bytes, with fields past the pinned IDL; the decoder reads the documented prefix and flags undocumented data.
- **Power v4 supersedes v3,** which is kept, labelled historical.

**Draft 4 (2026-10-07)** applied an eighth review:
- **the primary outcome is the account's economic P&L,** and it reconciles to cash. Unmeasured exits are impaired at zero recovery plus fees on the day the retry runs out. They count toward the daily risk budget, and their tokens stay quarantined. The bootstrap, the hurdle and the total all use this one series. The separate "conservative total" gate is gone, because it's now the same thing;
- **the quote is protocol-correct:** effective quote reserves include the pool's signed virtual quote reserves, and fees come from the fee configuration, not a hardcoded rate. There's a validity contract per quote;
- **qualification is a declared plan,** with a fixed window, a denominator and a confidence bound, not just "≥ 95% for two weeks";
- **the control's matching rules are written down;**
- **power v3:** the same statistic for every gate, and outages shared by both arms and evaluated at entry, exit and retry times. It also applies the INVALID rule, adds a block bootstrap, and records a full code fingerprint. The draft-3 table is superseded, and so is its claim that "nothing passes" at replay-like missingness: 2.7% and 1.3% of runs passed in two of its cells.

**Draft 3 (2026-10-07)** applied a seventh review:
- the signal is one shared, versioned implementation (`meme_trader/sniper/t9_signal.py`, `t9-signal-2`), whose boundary now matches this text;
- the account is one event-driven model (`meme_trader/sniper/t9_portfolio.py`), the same for the runner and the simulation;
- power is re-simulated through that model at H = 1 SOL, with realistic (17%, clustered) missingness and Monte Carlo intervals;
- the draft-2 power table is superseded.

**Draft 2** applied a sixth review's corrections (§6 A–F):
- power is simulated for the whole procedure;
- winner sizes are net returns;
- a fixed window with an operational-invalidation rule replaces the restartable clock;
- a registered execution and coverage treatment;
- an economic gate in SOL with its actual incremental cost;
- the signal frozen exactly as the corrected replay computes it.

Constants that change at registration are marked *proposed*.

## Hypothesis

On established PumpSwap coins, a sharp price-and-volume surge, entered 60 s after it's observed and held one hour, makes money after executable costs, beyond a matched control and cash. This holds for a fixed-size paper portfolio with deployment-like limits.

## Universe and what is recorded at the decision

- **Eligible pools:** PumpSwap pools whose coin is at least 2 days old, from the wallet recorder's established universe, with recorder liquidity ≥ $5k at the last snapshot before the decision (for universe accounting; the order gate is below).
- **No Mayhem coins.** The tri-state read must be "not Mayhem"; unknown is ineligible (fail-closed).
- **Feed state:** a decision made while the recorder is more than 60 s behind is skipped, and counted.
- **Recorded for every signal, taken or not:**
  - the pool's age and its source;
  - the liquidity snapshot (value and time);
  - the Mayhem state;
  - the feed's lag;
  - the reason for any rejection;
  - the control's selection inputs and seed;
  - the quote's timestamp and inputs;
  - every attempt (submitted, failed, unmeasured, filled).

## Signal (frozen: `meme_trader/sniper/t9_signal.py`, version `t9-signal-2`; its sha256 is frozen with this file)

At a check time *t*:
- **Price:** the trade price at *t* is ≥ 1.40× the last trade price at or before *t* − 300 s.
- **Volume:** v5 = SOL volume of trades with *t* − 300 ≤ ts ≤ *t*; v60 = SOL volume with *t* − 3,900 ≤ ts < *t* − 300 (60 minutes); surge = v5 ÷ (v60 ÷ 12) ≥ 4.
- **History:** the pool's first observed trade is ≥ 3,900 s before *t*, and trades exist at or before *t* − 300 and *t* − 900. There's no other minimum-observation gate.
- **Cadence:** a check runs at a pool's trade at least 30 s after its previous check.
- **Cooldown:** one signal per pool every 2 h.

This differs from draft 1 (55 min ÷ 11, plus a minimum of 10 trades) and from the exploratory forward test's boundaries.

- **Shared:** the replay (`t9_replay_v2.py`) calls this implementation, and the runner will too.
- **Boundaries tested:** a trade exactly at *t* − 300 or *t* − 900 is the earlier price; equal timestamps count up to the checked trade; the volume windows' edges; insufficient history (`tests/test_t9_signal.py`).
- **Version 1** (the first corrected replay) took the price strictly before *t* − 300. A seventh review found that disagreed with this text; its outputs are kept and labelled.

## Entry and execution

1. **Decision** at actual observation time (read time), never the trade's chain time.
2. **Wait** 60 s.
3. **Quote:** a protocol-exact PumpSwap quote for 0.25 SOL. See *The quote* below.
4. **Gate:** skip if impact > **1% per side** (*proposed*), separately from fees.
5. **Re-check:** Mayhem, the portfolio limits and the daily loss stop.
6. **Fill:** at that quote, with all fees and a priority fee per side. It's never filled at an earlier recorded print.

**A fresh quote isn't a guaranteed fill.** Waiting 60 s handles the signal's delay, not quote-to-land drift, failed transactions or exit liquidity. So:
- **Primary result:** at the fresh quote.
- **Unmeasured exits:** every exit that couldn't be measured is **impaired**: zero recovery plus its attributable fees, never a stale price.
  - It's booked on the UTC day the retry runs out, the day it's recognized.
  - This is part of the primary P&L and of cash.
- **The tokens stay as quarantined inventory.** A paper write-off isn't proof they're gone. The coin can't be entered again in the window.
- **Stress:** +1.5% cost per trade is reported beside it.

## The quote (validity contract)

- **Reserves.** Base reserves are the base vault's amount. Effective quote reserves are the quote vault's amount plus the pool's **signed** `virtual_quote_reserves`, which can be negative.
  - **Real accounts are longer than the IDL's struct.** Mainnet Pool accounts are 287–301 bytes on a 100-pool sample (2026-10-07), against the IDL's 270. pump.fun's README documents one more field (`is_holder_reward`), and 22% of pools carry further undocumented non-zero bytes. The documented prefix is read, and undocumented data is flagged and counted in qualification.
  - Older, shorter layouts are read with the missing fields as zero, but only when the account ends exactly at a field boundary. Anything else is a truncated account, and rejected.
  - **54% of sampled pools had a non-zero `virtual_quote_reserves`,** despite the README's "0 on all pools today". A vault-only quote would be wrong for half the universe.
  - Pump's public docs, pinned at commit `cb188ce`, define this.
- **Fees.**
  - Fees come from the fee program's configuration and tiers, read in the same context. The old 0.30% is never hardcoded.
  - Canonical and non-canonical pools are treated as the pinned SDK/IDL version specifies.
- **The read.**
  - Pool, vaults, mints and fee accounts come from one `getMultipleAccounts` context where possible.
  - A discovery read followed by a batch re-checks that the pool still points at those accounts.
  - `minContextSlot` is a lower bound, not a snapshot request.
- **Math.**
  - Integer atoms, with the protocol's rounding.
  - Impact is kept apart from fees, and priority and network costs apart from both.
  - An unsupported mint extension is rejected (fail-closed).
- **Freshness (*proposed*, to calibrate).**
  - The response is ≤ 5 s old when used, and ≤ 2 slots behind a reference at the same commitment.
  - Both clocks are recorded.
- **Saved per quote:**
  - request and response times;
  - the context slot and the reference slot's lag;
  - the program, account owners, layout, addresses, decimals and token program;
  - the raw, virtual and effective reserves;
  - the fee and config accounts;
  - the integer input and output;
  - the code and SDK hashes;
  - the reason for any rejection.
- **Golden fixtures** must match the pinned SDK:
  - buys and sells, at small and large sizes;
  - positive, zero and negative virtual reserves;
  - fee-tier boundaries, low liquidity, and both token programs.
- **A quote isn't a fill,** and simulating a transaction doesn't guarantee it later lands.
- **No quote** (timeout, a node behind, a missing account, an unsupported layout, a migrated route, no liquidity, a frozen token):
  - classify it, and retry on the fixed clock, with a limited check on an independent provider;
  - a permitted migration route found with information available at that time is quoted on its own;
  - otherwise the position is execution-unmeasured. A last valid quote is a sensitivity, never the primary value.

## Qualifying the quote (before registering)

- **A declared plan,** written before it starts:
  - a fixed window of at least 14 full days, with at least 200 distinct planned exits, reporting clustering by day and coin;
  - **the denominator** is every eligible planned exit, not only pools that answered. Early skips are reported separately;
  - **the target:** the lower 95% confidence bound of timely valid quotes is ≥ 95%. A 95% point estimate isn't enough.
- **The window must include:**
  - cold starts and restarts, quiet pools and volatile ones;
  - both the primary and the control arm;
  - provider failures and midnights.
- **Also required:**
  - the protocol fixtures pass, and serialized quote inputs reproduce;
  - each quote's move against the next state is recorded.
- **A window that falls short is "not qualified."** A new threshold or a longer window is a new, logged qualification plan.
- **Qualification data never enters the confirmatory window.**

## Exit

- **When:** one hour after the entry fill. Only this exit is primary, with no alternatives.
- **How:** a fresh quote, filled at that quote.
- **Pool unavailable or no valid quote:** retry every 30 s for 15 min, then *execution-unmeasured*.
- **Last quote and zero are sensitivity scenarios,** not bounds on every possible loss.

## Portfolio

- **Account:** an independent 9 SOL paper account, with at most 4 open positions and one per coin.
- **Daily risk budget:** 0.5 SOL of risk losses in a UTC day stops new entries for that day; exits continue.
  - Risk losses are measured losses plus impairments, each on its own day.
  - Each exit that has failed and is still being retried also reserves its potential impairment against the budget.
- **Isolation:** this account doesn't touch, reset or bypass the main bot's book or kill switch.

## Control and baselines

For each signal, **one primary matched non-signal pool** is chosen at the decision, from the eligible universe as snapshotted then. Two more may be recorded as diagnostics, chosen the same way; they never get their own bankroll, and the best-looking one is never picked afterwards.

- **Matched on** (calipers and ranking predeclared):
  - the same protocol and quote-token class, with valid gates (no Mayhem coins);
  - similar age and executable impact at the 0.25 SOL stake;
  - similar pre-decision activity and volatility **before the surge**. The surge itself is the feature under test, so it's never matched away;
  - a similar quote-health regime and fee tier.
- **Only feature versions available at decision time** are used.
- **Selection:** one candidate is chosen with a recorded seed from the closest eligible ones. The pool IDs, the seed and the distances are saved.
  - No candidate within the calipers means the signal is **unmatched**, and that's recorded. The calipers are never loosened afterwards.
  - A control stays a control even if it later becomes a signal.
- **The control's account:** the same clocks, quote rules, missing-state treatment, costs and risk rules, on a separate identical bankroll, chronologically. If the control's account would skip a trade, it skips it.
- **Reported:** balance diagnostics and how often signals were unmatched. Matched-pair diagnostics are secondary to the portfolio difference.
- **Cash** (zero) is the second baseline.

## Window, outages and invalidation

- **Fixed dates:** *proposed* **90** complete UTC days (*not chosen yet*: it's fixed from power with estimated availability, after qualification) from the registration's start date (the first UTC midnight after the frozen code is deployed). Every day counts, including zero-trade days and downtime.
  - Why 90, not draft 1's 60: see Power.
  - Day 45 is an operational and data-quality review only: no performance verdict, no changes.
- **Downtime** (feed, recorder or runner) is reported per day, with what was exposed during it.
- **Operational invalidation, declared now:**
  - **When:** the runner observes less than 80% of the window's hours, or more than 20% of attempted trades end execution-unmeasured.
  - **Then:** the run ends INVALID. Its intervals and outcomes are kept and reported.
  - **Any new run** is a new registration (T9-E1b) on a later, untouched window.
  - **Never:** the clock isn't restarted, extended or rolled forward.

## Verdict

- **Primary outcome:** the account's **economic daily P&L** (`t9_portfolio.Portfolio.daily`), chronological and intention-to-trade.
  - It's measured exits plus impairments, after all costs.
  - It reconciles to cash: with nothing open, its total equals cash minus the bankroll.
  - The measured-only series is a diagnostic, labelled so, and is never used for a gate.
- **PASS** requires all four, on that one series:
  1. The day bootstrap's 5th percentile of the mean daily P&L is > 0.
  2. The same for the mean daily difference against the matched control.
  3. Total economic P&L ≥ **H** (below) over the window.
  4. Measurement coverage ≥ 80% of attempted trades. Below that, the run is INVALID anyway, see above.
- **A 3-day moving-block bootstrap** of the same statistics is reported beside the registered one-day bootstrap, as a sensitivity.
- **FAIL:** the 95th percentile of the mean daily P&L is ≤ 0, or the total is below the control's.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** anything else. It's never extended until it wins.
- **Report:**
  - winner concentration by distinct coin and episode, and by day;
  - leave-one-day-out and leave-one-episode-out results;
  - the largest winner's and the top 3's share;
  - drawdown and time in market;
  - fill rate and coverage;
  - the stress.

**Economic gate H.**
- **This test's incremental cost is $0.** It runs on the feed and RPC already paid for, and needs no faster infrastructure (it acts in minutes).
- **So H is not an infrastructure bill.** It's the owner's materiality threshold, stated in SOL so no exchange rate is needed: *proposed* **1.0 SOL** over the window, about +11% on the 9 SOL account. The owner sets it before the freeze.
- **For a later live version:** its hurdle adds its actual incremental costs (priority fees are already inside P&L; any new subscription in USD is converted at the frozen rate on its registration day).

## Power v6 and the risk policy (an eleventh review, 2026-10-07): a decision for the owner

**The question:** the registered daily stop (0.5 SOL of gross losses halts entries) is an entry trigger, not a maximum daily loss. Four fresh positions can lose 1.0 SOL, and its median worst day in power v6 is about −0.8 SOL.

**What v6 compared:** three predeclared policies on common random numbers (`data/research/power_t9_e1_v6.json`):

| Policy | What it does | Effect on power |
|---|---|---|
| gross (as registered) | halts entries once the day's gross losses reach 0.5 SOL | the baseline |
| hard | a strict worst-case budget: the day can't lose more than 0.5 SOL | one position at a time; up to −18 points |
| net+cap | a net-loss stop with a 1.0 SOL gross cap | up to +20 points at about the same false-pass rate, but larger losses and drawdowns with no edge |

**Before any window is chosen:**
- the owner picks the policy (or another budget);
- costs are calibrated: at +2.8 points of cost, a ~4% round trip, power falls sharply.

The reviewer advised keeping the gross baseline unless the owner's risk tolerance says otherwise. Drift sensitivities, now paired, change nothing.

## Power v5 (a tenth review, 2026-10-07)

**What changed:** version 5 makes the late-fill price move mean-preserving (v4's exp(σZ) added a positive drift) and counts the late-fill share over executed exits.
- **The result:** the centered results equal the v4 table below within simulation noise, and the drift sensitivities move pass rates by a few points either way (`data/research/power_t9_e1_v5.json`).
- **New: the daily loss stop limits capacity.** Its per-arm counts show the 0.5 SOL stop, which counts gross losses, skips 56% of signals at A3 with the replay's winner rate.
- **An open design question:** a net-loss stop, or a larger budget, should be decided before a window is chosen.

## Power: the whole procedure through the account model (`research/power_t9_e1.py` v4, seeded)

**What's simulated:**
- **The account:** each test runs every signal through `t9_portfolio.Portfolio`, the runner's own account model.
- **Exits** are simulated attempt by attempt on the 30 s retry clock and fill at the **first valid quote**, at the return then. An exit with no quote within the retries is impaired.
- **Availability:**
  - a shared provider outage calendar, plus rare long outages;
  - transient failures that block a pool for 30–300 s;
  - pools that can't be quoted at all, independent of the trade's return. The informative version (collapsing pools fail more often) is a separate, labelled stress.
- **INVALID** if observed hours are below 80%, or **either** arm's coverage is below 80%.

**The availability scenarios** are the reviewer's planning envelope, as a small joint grid frozen before the run. They're engineering stress cases, **not estimates of any provider**:

| | mean time between outages | typical outage | long outages | transient failure per attempt | unquotable pools |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | 168 h | 30 s | none | 1% | 0.5% |
| A2 | 48 h | 5 min | 4 h, monthly | 5% | 1% |
| A3 | 24 h | 5 min | 8 h, monthly | 10% | 3% |
| A4 | 12 h | 30 min | 8 h, weekly | 10% | 3% |
| A5 | 6 h | 2 h | 24 h, weekly | 20% | 8% |

| Pass rate at 90 days (H = 1 SOL, +0 cost) | A1 | A2 | A3 | A4 | A5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **No edge** (breakeven after costs; 500 runs) | 0.020 [0.011, 0.036] | 0.004 | 0.000 | 0.000 | always INVALID |
| An eighth of the replay's winner rate, flat ordinary trades | 0.29 | 0.13 | 0.00 | 0.00 | — |
| A quarter, flat | 0.70 | 0.59 | 0.23 | 0.01 | — |
| Half, flat | 0.99 | 0.95 | 0.77 | 0.36 | — |
| Half, pessimistic ordinary trades | 0.33 | 0.20 | 0.08 | 0.05 | — |
| Half, flat, +1.5% cost | 0.93 | 0.80 | 0.59 | 0.22 | — |
| The replay's rate, flat | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 0.97 | — |
| The replay's rate, pessimistic | 0.97 | 0.97 | 0.83 | 0.56 | — |
| The replay's rate, winners halved, flat | 1.00 | 0.99 | 0.91 | 0.50 | — |
| *(median exit coverage, the replay's rate, flat)* | 0.995 | 0.989 | 0.968 | 0.944 | 0.81; 65% of hours observed |

**What this says:**
- **False passes stay inside the nominal 5%.** The highest is 2.6%, at A1 with no extra cost over 60 days. A breakeven null sits exactly on the boundary of a one-sided 5% test, so a rate of a few percent is expected there.
- **Availability decides.**
  - From A2 (~99% of exits measured) to A3 (~97%), half the replay's rate drops from 0.95 to 0.77, and to 0.36 at A4 (~94%).
  - About 10% of exits fill late at A3: they hold cash and reserve risk meanwhile.
- **The informative stress costs a little more:** half the rate at A3 drops from 0.77 to 0.71.
- **Window length matters less than availability.** A quarter of the rate, flat, passes at A1 0.53 / 0.70 / 0.79 and at A2 0.45 / 0.59 / 0.69, over 60 / 90 / 120 days.
- **The full table:** `data/research/power_t9_e1_v4.{out,json}` (858 cells), from revision `256177a` with no uncommitted changes. Its sha256 is in `.sha256`.
- **v3's table is superseded** and kept as historical: its exit clock was wrong.

**So, before registering:**
1. Build the live quote observer on `pumpswap.py` (see *The quote*).
2. Qualify it under a declared plan (see *Qualifying the quote*), for both arms.
3. Re-run power with availability **estimated** from its reason-coded logs, which replace A1–A5. Then choose and freeze the window and H from a conservative scenario, before any confirmatory data.

**Two more limits:**
- **Model results only.** Real matched controls, real outages and the real winner rate may differ.
- **Not a test of the edge itself.** An INCONCLUSIVE or INVALID verdict isn't evidence of no edge.

## What invalidates it

- **Any change after the freeze** to the rules, costs, quote model, control selection or code paths this test uses makes it a new version. A safety fix that changes behaviour does too.
- **Operational invalidation,** as defined above, ends the run INVALID. The run is kept and never replaced by restarting it.
