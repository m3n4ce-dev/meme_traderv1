# T9-E1: second-life momentum, one executable configuration (PROPOSED)

**Status: proposed, not registered.** It needs the owner's approval. Once approved, this file is frozen (hash-locked like `research/policies/*`), and any material change starts T9-E2 with its own untouched period. The existing forward test (`revival.py`, v2, 18 variants) stays as it is, **exploratory**: its numbers are never quoted as confirmation of this one.

The design comes from a fifth external review (2026-10-07). The constants that change at registration are marked *proposed*.

## Hypothesis

On established PumpSwap coins, a sharp price-and-volume surge, entered 60 s after it's observed and held one hour, makes money after executable costs, beyond a matched control and cash. This holds for a fixed-size paper portfolio with deployment-like limits.

## Universe (as of the decision)

- PumpSwap pools whose coin is at least 2 days old, from the wallet recorder's established universe. Age comes from the pool's first-seen or the coin's creation time; the source is recorded.
- Recorder liquidity ≥ $5k at the last snapshot before the decision. This is for universe accounting only; the order gate is below.
- **No Mayhem coins.** The tri-state read must be "not Mayhem"; unknown is ineligible (fail-closed).
- Feed state recorded. A decision made while the recorder is more than 60 s behind is skipped, and counted.

## Signal

- **Price:** the last trade price is ≥ 1.40× the last price at or before t − 300 s.
- **Volume:** the volume in (t − 300 s, t] is ≥ 4 × (volume in (t − 3600 s, t − 300 s] ÷ 11). Those are the preceding 55 minutes, excluding the current 5. (The review's wording "one twelfth of the preceding 60 minutes excluding the current 5" means 55 minutes; it is stated here exactly.)
- **Minimum observations:** ≥ 10 trades in the current 5 minutes, and ≥ 1 trade before t − 300 s.
- **Cadence:** checked every 30 s of a pool's trading.
- **Cooldown:** one signal per pool every 2 h.

## Entry

1. **Decision** at actual observation time (read time), never at the trade's chain time.
2. **Wait** 60 s.
3. **Quote:** read the pool's vault balances on-chain (fresh, ≤ 5 s old) and compute the constant-product quote for 0.25 SOL, including PumpSwap fees.
4. **Gate:** skip if impact > **1% per side** (*proposed*), separately from fees.
5. **Re-check:** Mayhem, the portfolio limits and the daily loss stop.
6. **Fill:** at that quote. It is never filled at an earlier recorded print.

## Size and costs

- **Size:** 0.25 SOL fixed.
- **Costs:** every fee is charged (LP, protocol and creator fees as read from the pool), plus a priority fee per side.
- **Stress:** the result is also reported at +1.5% extra cost per trade.

## Exit

- **When:** one hour after the entry fill. Only this exit is primary, with no alternatives.
- **How:** a fresh quote, filled at that quote.
- **Pool unavailable or no valid quote:** retry every 30 s for up to 15 min, then mark the trade *execution-unmeasured*. It's reported separately, with conservative bounds: the last valid quote, and zero.
- **No stale-mark profits:** a stale price never counts as a gain.

## Portfolio

- **Account:** an independent 9 SOL paper account, with at most 4 open positions and one per coin.
- **Daily loss stop:** realized losses of 0.5 SOL in a UTC day stop new entries for that day; exits continue.
- **Isolation:** this account doesn't touch, reset or bypass the main bot's book or kill switch.

## Control and baselines

For each signal, one **matched non-signal pool** is chosen at the decision, using only pre-signal data:
- the same liquidity bucket (×2 bands) and age bucket;
- activity in the last 55 minutes within ×2 of the signal pool's.

It is chosen with a recorded seed and run with the same clocks, gates, costs, exit and a separate identical bankroll. **Cash** (zero) is the second baseline.

## Duration and verdict

- **Primary horizon:** 60 complete UTC days from registration. Day 28 is an operational and data-quality review only: no performance verdict, no changes.
- **Why 60 days, and why it can be inconclusive:** `research/power_t9.py` (seeded; summary below).
- **Primary outcome:** chronological net portfolio P&L after all costs, every day included (zero-trade days too). It is reported intention-to-trade: signals, attempts, fills and skips each counted.
- **Pass:** all three of the following.
  1. The day-clustered bootstrap's 5th percentile of the mean daily P&L is > 0.
  2. The total beats the matched control's total by its own bootstrap 5th percentile > 0.
  3. Net P&L is ≥ 1.0 SOL over the 60 days (*proposed*), covering about two months of a paid feed or server. This is the economic hurdle.
- **Fail:** the 95th percentile is ≤ 0, or the point estimate is below the control.
- **Otherwise:** **inconclusive**, never extended until it wins.
- **Report:** winner concentration by distinct coin and day; leave-one-day-out and leave-one-episode-out results; the largest winner's and the top 3's share; drawdown; time in market; fill rate; stressed costs.

## Power (from `research/power_t9.py`)

**Model** (from the exploratory replay):
- An ordinary trade has median −9.6% and mean −0.3%.
- With probability p a trade is a revival of +M.
- A test passes when the day-clustered 5th percentile is > 0.

**Results:**
- **Calibration:** with no edge, the test passes 4–9% of the time.
- **Large edges:** at the replay's own estimate (p ≈ 2%, M ≈ 9×, which is optimistic, being the best rule chosen on the same three days), power is ≥ 0.76 even at 28 days.
- **A winner every 100 trades at +500%** (+5.7% EV per trade), over 60 days:
  - 10 trades a day: power 0.63 (0.36 with stress costs);
  - 25 trades a day: power 0.93 (0.71 with stress costs).
- **Small edges** (EV ≈ +1–3% per trade) are underpowered even at 120 days. So a fail or an inconclusive result at 60 days rules out large edges, not small ones.

## What invalidates it

- Any change to the rules, costs, quote model, control selection or code paths this test uses, after the freeze, makes it a new version. A safety fix that changes behaviour does too.
- A feed outage longer than 2 h stops and restarts the clock, and is reported.
